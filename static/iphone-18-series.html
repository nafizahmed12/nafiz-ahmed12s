iPhone 18 Series Price in Bangladesh

Updated September 10, 2026: Apple has officially announced the iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max. This hub focuses on confirmed launch information and Bangladesh buying intent rather than pre-launch rumors.

Official launch update

iPhone 18 Pro and iPhone 18 Pro Max were announced by Apple on September 9, 2026. Pre-orders begin September 12 and availability begins September 18 in the first launch markets.

iPhone 18 Pro and Pro Max

Both Pro models use Apple's A20 Pro chip and introduce a 48MP Fusion Main camera with variable aperture, along with a smaller Dynamic Island and iOS 27 with Apple Intelligence and Siri AI. Storage options are 256GB, 512GB, 1TB and 2TB, with black, silver, glacier and burgundy finishes.

iPhone 18 Pro Price in Bangladesh

Apple's U.S. starting price is $1,199 for iPhone 18 Pro. The Bangladesh retail price depends on local taxes, import costs, storage, region/model number and warranty, so check the final local invoice before buying.

iPhone 18 Pro Max Price in Bangladesh

Apple's U.S. starting price is $1,299 for iPhone 18 Pro Max. Bangladesh pricing can differ substantially by storage, seller, region/model and warranty. Do not treat unofficial leaked prices as final.

What about the standard iPhone 18?

The September 2026 Apple event focused on the Pro lineup and did not announce the standard iPhone 18. Keep the standard-model page clearly separated from confirmed Pro launch information until Apple publishes an official announcement.

Bangladesh buying checklist

Before ordering, confirm seller identity, exact model/region, storage, warranty coverage, VAT/invoice, SIM configuration, return policy, delivery terms and final price.
