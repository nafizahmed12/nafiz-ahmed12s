Refund & Return Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Digital products

Because digital products can normally be accessed or delivered immediately, purchases are generally non-refundable after successful delivery or download.

Technical or access problems

If a purchased digital product is materially inaccessible, corrupted, or not delivered because of a technical problem on our side, contact us with the order details. We will investigate and, where appropriate, provide access, a replacement or a refund.

Physical products

Where physical products are offered, any applicable return or refund conditions will be shown with the product or order information. Items may need to be returned in an eligible condition.

Payment issues

If a payment was charged but an order was not successfully completed, contact support with the transaction or order information so we can investigate the payment status.

How to request help

Use our Contact page and include your order information and a clear description of the issue. Do not send full card numbers or passwords.
