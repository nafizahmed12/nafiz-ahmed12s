Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 9, 2026

Nafiz Ecommerce respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, why we use it, and how cookies and third-party services may operate.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect your name, email address, account information, order information, messages sent to customer support, subscription information, and technical information needed to keep the website secure and functional.

How we use information

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To process orders, payments, refunds and customer-support requests.
  • To provide requested services and product information.
  • To protect the website against abuse, fraud and unauthorized access.
  • To improve website performance, usability and security.
  • To send updates when you have voluntarily subscribed or otherwise requested them.

Payments

Payment details are processed through the payment providers presented during checkout. We do not intentionally store full payment-card credentials on our application servers. Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies or similar session technologies where necessary for login sessions, security, forms, shopping-cart functionality and other core features. Third-party services may also use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers according to their own policies.

Google advertising and AdSense

If Google AdSense or another Google advertising service is enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve, personalize, measure and limit advertising, including based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Third parties may place and read cookies on a user's browser and may use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers as part of ad serving.

Users can manage personalized advertising choices through Google Ads Settings. Users may also learn more about Google's use of data on partner sites through How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Where legally required, we will use an appropriate consent mechanism and respect applicable privacy choices before serving personalized advertising.

Third-party services and links

Our website may link to third-party payment providers, retailers, affiliate services, analytics services or other external websites. Those services operate under their own privacy policies. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of external websites.

Affiliate recommendations

Some product recommendations may use affiliate links. When an affiliate relationship applies, it will be disclosed on the relevant page. Affiliate partners may receive information when you follow their links or complete a purchase on their websites.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including secure sessions, CSRF protection and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and your choices

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, security and operational requirements. You may contact us to ask about your personal information or request correction or deletion where applicable and legally permitted.

Children

This website is not intentionally directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for advertising purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technologies or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.

Contact

For privacy questions, use our Contact page.
