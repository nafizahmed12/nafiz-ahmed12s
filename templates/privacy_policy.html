Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 27, 2026

Nafiz Commerce respects your privacy. This policy explains the types of information we may collect and how we use it to operate and improve the website.

Information we collect

When you create an account, place an order, subscribe to updates or contact us, we may collect information such as your name, email address, account details, order details and messages you submit.

How we use information

We use information to provide accounts and services, process orders and payments, deliver digital products where applicable, provide support, prevent abuse and improve the website.

Payments

Payment details are processed through the payment providers presented during checkout. We do not intentionally store full payment card credentials on our application servers.

Cookies and similar technologies

We may use essential cookies or session technologies to keep you signed in, protect forms and maintain website functionality. Third-party services may use their own technologies according to their policies.

Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are enabled, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to serve and measure ads, subject to their applicable policies and your available controls.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including secure sessions, CSRF protection and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Your choices

You can contact us regarding account information or privacy questions. Where applicable, you may request correction or deletion of information subject to legal and operational requirements.

Contact

For privacy questions, use our Contact page.
