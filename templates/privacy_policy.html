Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 13, 2026

Nafiz Ecommerce respects your privacy. This policy explains what information may be collected when you use our website, why we use it, and how cookies, advertising technologies and third-party services may operate.

Information we collect

Depending on how you use the website, we may collect your name, email address, account information, order information, customer-support messages, subscription information, and technical information needed to keep the website secure and functional. Technical information may include IP address, browser/device information, approximate usage information, and server or security logs.

How we use information

  • To create and maintain user accounts.
  • To process orders, payments, refunds and support requests.
  • To provide requested services and product information.
  • To protect the website against abuse, fraud and unauthorized access.
  • To improve website performance, usability and security.
  • To send updates when you have voluntarily subscribed or requested them.

Payments

Payment details are processed through the payment providers presented during checkout. We do not intentionally store full payment-card credentials on our application servers. Payment providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

Cookies and similar technologies

We use essential cookies or similar session technologies where necessary for login sessions, security, forms, shopping-cart functionality and other core features. Third-party services may also use cookies, web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers according to their own policies. These technologies may be used for authentication, security, analytics, advertising, measurement, fraud prevention or other service functions, depending on which services are enabled.

Google advertising and AdSense

If Google AdSense or another Google advertising service is enabled on this website, Google and its advertising partners may use cookies and similar technologies to serve, personalize, measure and limit advertising, including based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Third parties may place and read cookies on a user's browser and may use web beacons, IP addresses or similar identifiers as part of ad serving.

Google may use advertising cookies to help serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ads Settings. Users can also learn more about Google's use of information from partner sites through How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

If other third-party advertising vendors or ad networks are enabled, this policy will be updated as appropriate to identify those vendors and provide relevant information about their privacy choices or opt-out mechanisms. Where legally required, we will use an appropriate consent mechanism and respect applicable privacy choices before serving personalized advertising.

Third-party services and links

Our website may link to payment providers, retailers, affiliate services, analytics services, advertising providers or other external websites. Those services may collect or process information such as cookies, IP addresses, device/browser information or activity data according to their own privacy policies. We do not control the privacy practices of external websites. Please review the applicable third party's privacy policy and available privacy choices before using its services.

Affiliate recommendations

Some product recommendations may use affiliate links. When an affiliate relationship applies, it will be disclosed on the relevant page. Affiliate partners may receive information when you follow their links or complete a purchase on their websites. Affiliate partners operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

Data sharing

We may share information with service providers that help us operate the website, such as payment processors, hosting providers, security providers, customer-support services, analytics providers, advertising providers and affiliate or retail partners, when necessary for the relevant service or transaction. We may also disclose information when required by law, to protect rights and safety, or to prevent fraud and abuse.

Data security

We use reasonable technical and organizational safeguards, including secure sessions, CSRF protection and access controls. No internet service can guarantee absolute security.

Data retention and your choices

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including legal, accounting, security and operational requirements. You may contact us to ask about your personal information or request correction or deletion where applicable and legally permitted. You can also manage browser cookie controls and, where available, personalized advertising choices through the relevant third-party provider.

Children

This website is not intentionally directed to children under the minimum age required by applicable law. We do not knowingly request personal information from children for advertising purposes.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when our services, technologies or legal obligations change. The latest version will be published on this page with an updated date.
