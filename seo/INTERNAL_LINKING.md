# Internal Linking Plan

## Primary topic cluster
- `/iphone-18-series` — iPhone 18 series hub
- `/iphone-18` — standard model research intent
- `/iphone-18-pro` — Pro model research intent
- `/iphone-18-pro-max` — Pro Max model research intent
- `/iphone-18-comparison` — comparison intent

## Supporting commercial destinations
- `/shop?category=mobile` — mobile shopping category
- `/shop?category=accessories` — accessories shopping category
- `/shop` — full catalog

## Linking rules
1. The iPhone 18 hub links to every model page and the comparison page.
2. The hub also links to relevant commercial mobile/accessory destinations.
3. Model pages should link back to the hub and comparison page where contextually useful.
4. Commercial pages should link to relevant informational guides when the link helps the shopper.
5. Use descriptive anchor text rather than generic labels such as “click here”.
6. Keep links visible and useful to visitors; do not add hidden SEO-only links.
