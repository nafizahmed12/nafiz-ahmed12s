# iPhone 18 Pro / Pro Max Google Ranking Strategy

## Goal
Build two strong, non-competing landing pages that target Bangladesh purchase/research intent while supporting the broader iPhone 18 topic cluster.

## Page ownership

| URL | Primary keyword | Search intent | Secondary keywords |
|---|---|---|---|
| `/iphone-18-pro` | iPhone 18 Pro price in Bangladesh | Commercial research | iPhone 18 Pro price Bangladesh, iPhone 18 Pro specs, iPhone 18 Pro release date, iPhone 18 Pro features, iPhone 18 Pro camera |
| `/iphone-18-pro-max` | iPhone 18 Pro Max price in Bangladesh | Commercial research | iPhone 18 Pro Max price Bangladesh, iPhone 18 Pro Max specs, iPhone 18 Pro Max release date, iPhone 18 Pro Max camera, iPhone 18 Pro Max battery |

Do not make the two pages compete for the same primary keyword. The Pro Max page owns Pro Max queries; the Pro page owns Pro queries.

## Recommended content structure

Each page should have:
1. A concise answer-first introduction with current launch/availability status.
2. Current official price or clearly labelled local-price status; never present an estimated/leaked Bangladesh price as official.
3. A scannable specifications table.
4. Camera, display, chip, battery, storage and connectivity sections with unique useful commentary.
5. A Bangladesh buying section covering warranty, region/model, VAT/invoice and delivery considerations.
6. A direct comparison link to the other Pro model.
7. FAQ covering price, release/availability, storage and key differences.
8. A visible "last updated" date and a source/verification note when facts change quickly.

## Content differentiation

Avoid publishing near-duplicate pages for every keyword variation. Expand the existing pages with genuinely useful information, original comparisons, screenshots/photos where available, and Bangladesh-specific buying guidance. Google warns against keyword stuffing, link spam and scaled low-value content.

## Backlink strategy

### Tier 1 — highest priority
Earn editorial links from relevant, legitimate sources:
- Bangladesh technology/news publications
- Established mobile/consumer-tech blogs
- Local buying guides that genuinely review or compare phones
- University/student technology publications when the article is genuinely relevant
- Your own social/video content that naturally references the page

Pitch useful original assets rather than asking for keyword-stuffed links. Examples:
- Bangladesh iPhone price tracker
- iPhone 18 Pro vs Pro Max comparison table
- Storage/value calculator
- Original launch-day specification summary
- Bangladesh availability/warranty guide

### Tier 2 — supporting links
- Relevant community discussions where the page directly answers the question
- Genuine business/profile citations where the business information is legitimate
- Relevant local directories only when they provide real user value
- Manufacturer/product resources for factual reference (do not ask for manipulative backlinks)

### Avoid
- Paid links intended to pass ranking credit
- Bulk guest-post networks
- Automated backlink services
- Spam comments/forums
- PBNs and expired-domain link schemes
- Excessive exact-match anchor text
- Reciprocal link schemes created mainly for ranking manipulation

## Anchor-text mix

Keep anchors natural and varied:
- 30–40% branded/navigational: "Nafiz Store", "Nafiz Store iPhone guide"
- 30–40% partial-match/contextual: "iPhone 18 Pro details", "Pro Max buying guide"
- 10–20% exact-match: "iPhone 18 Pro price in Bangladesh", "iPhone 18 Pro Max price in Bangladesh"
- Remaining: generic/natural anchors such as "this guide" or the bare URL

These are planning ranges, not quotas. Never force an anchor distribution; relevance and editorial context matter more.

## Technical SEO checklist

- Keep one canonical URL per page.
- Keep both URLs in the XML sitemap.
- Ensure pages return HTTP 200 and are crawlable.
- Ensure important SEO copy is present in initial HTML where practical.
- Validate Product structured data against Google's current requirements.
- Use accurate price/availability values when an Offer is shown; do not mark up invented prices.
- Use ProductGroup/variant markup if the site exposes separate indexable storage/color URLs.
- Add meaningful image alt text.
- Ensure fast mobile rendering and stable Core Web Vitals.
- Submit/refresh the sitemap in Google Search Console after major updates.

## Measurement

Track separately in Google Search Console:
- `/iphone-18-pro`
- `/iphone-18-pro-max`

Monitor queries, impressions, CTR, average position, indexing status and cannibalization. Review weekly for the first month and monthly afterward.

## Success target

The first objective is not an immediate #1 position. The sequence is:
1. Get both URLs indexed.
2. Establish impressions for the primary and long-tail queries.
3. Improve CTR with accurate titles/descriptions.
4. Build topical relevance through useful supporting content and internal links.
5. Earn a small number of strong, relevant editorial backlinks.
6. Refresh facts promptly as official/local information changes.

## Source verification

For launch facts, use Apple's official newsroom/product information as the primary factual source. For search appearance and structured-data implementation, follow Google Search Central documentation.
