# Ranking & Keyword Targeting

This phase maps one primary search intent to each existing iPhone 18 SEO page.

## Primary targets

| Page | Primary intent | Supporting terms |
|---|---|---|
| `/iphone-18` | iPhone 18 price in Bangladesh | specs, release date, expected price |
| `/iphone-18-pro` | iPhone 18 Pro price in Bangladesh | specs, release date, expected price |
| `/iphone-18-pro-max` | iPhone 18 Pro Max price in Bangladesh | specs, release date, expected price |
| `/iphone-18-series` | iPhone 18 series price in Bangladesh | lineup, models, series comparison |
| `/iphone-18-comparison` | iPhone 18 vs 18 Pro vs 18 Pro Max | comparison, which model to buy |

## Rules

- Keep each page focused on its own search intent.
- Use keywords naturally in titles, headings, introductory copy and relevant internal links.
- Avoid repetitive keyword lists or unnatural repetition.
- Do not create duplicate pages for near-identical queries.
- Do not publish unverified specifications or prices as facts.
- Update factual claims when reliable official or local-market information changes.

## Why this structure

The existing pages already have clean canonical URLs and a connected internal-link cluster. This phase strengthens topical targeting without creating doorway pages or keyword-stuffed content. Google explicitly warns against keyword stuffing, doorway abuse and scaled low-value pages, so the strategy prioritizes useful, distinct pages instead.
