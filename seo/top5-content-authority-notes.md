# Top-5 SEO Content Authority Plan

## Primary intent
Build useful Bangladesh-focused reference content around the iPhone 18 series without keyword stuffing, copied content, or unsupported product claims.

## Current priorities
- Keep public clean URLs as canonical URLs.
- Make important structured data available in initial HTML.
- Clearly label rumored/unconfirmed specifications.
- Add genuinely useful Bangladesh buying checks: storage, region/model number, warranty, invoice/VAT, delivery and official availability.
- Strengthen contextual internal links between the series hub and model pages.
- Use Search Console performance data to prioritize queries and pages after indexing.

## Guardrails
- Do not invent official prices, launch dates, specifications, reviews, ratings or stock status.
- Do not use FAQ markup with the expectation of FAQ rich results; Google retired that Search feature in 2026.
- Do not create doorway pages or repetitive keyword-only pages.
- Preserve the SEO registry and use a separate branch/PR for future topic pages.
